Features

Thoughtful by default

Celebration software fails when it’s careless. Confetti is built around the judgment calls a good People Ops person already makes.

Sensitive moments, handled gently

Office relationships, illness, losses. Confetti can collect awkward and private information discreetly and hand you the context directly. Handle sensitive moments discreetly and with care.

Context that makes it personal

When someone joins, Confetti syncs with your HRIS and asks about their hobbies, where they’re from, what they’re working on. Every celebration and event is tailored to the actual person, not a one-size-fits-all template.

Runs company-wide events

Volunteer days, game nights, offsites, and nerf wars: Confetti proposes them, finds the date with the highest in-office overlap, books what’s needed, and rallies signups. No fourteen-reply scheduling thread.

Slack-native from minute one

Install with Slack OAuth, try it in a private sandbox channel, then go live with /confetti setup. No new tool for your team to learn. Confetti lives where they already are.
